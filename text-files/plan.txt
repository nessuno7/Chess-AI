1) board encoding:
taking inspiration from AlphaZero 8x8x119 tensors, I will implement a reduced version 
of the 8x8x119
AlphaZero:
for each timestep 6 planes for white pieces, 6 planes for black pieces and 2 planes for repetition count
this was stored for the current timstep and the previous 7, giving 8 x 14 = 112 8x8 planes, 
the last 7 planes are so called global constants:
colour, move count, castling ×4, no-progress counter

For this project, I will make the number of previous timesteps a hyperparamter to fine tune the model later on

2) legal move masking:
will again use the alpha zero legal move masking consiting of an aarray containing all possible moves, including all possible promotions
